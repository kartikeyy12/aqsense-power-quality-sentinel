AQSENSE · Facility Twin

software application driven by LTspice outputs of the analog sentinel — simulation only

AQSENSE circuit says (LTspice nodes)

–
LED1 >180 V COMP1_OUT low
LED2 >200 V COMP2_OUT low
LED3 >240 V COMP3_OUT low
LED4 >260 V COMP4_OUT low
Buzzer BUZZER_OUT (555)
–
LOW FREQ_LO low
OK FREQ_OK_N low
HIGH FREQ_HI low
LED brightness = fraction of each 20 ms the LTspice node was low, so comparator chatter near a threshold is visible, not hidden. Mains/frequency estimates are calibrated read-outs of VSENSE and FREQ_V; the alarm decisions are the circuit's.

Virtual facility (software supervisor reacting to AQSENSE flags)

 

Out-of-spec exposure (s)

Exposure = time the applied mains (recorded in LTspice) was outside 180-260 V / 49-51 Hz while the asset was still connected. Includes the circuit's own detection latency and threshold offsets.

Readouts

Est. values are calibrated from VSENSE and FREQ_V. Applied values are recorded in LTspice. Shading shows the fraction of each 20 ms the node was low. All alarm decisions come from the circuit.

Facility

Untick Supervisor actions to show the facility ignoring the AQSENSE flags.

Exposure, seconds

Time the applied mains was outside 180 to 260 V or 49 to 51 Hz while the asset was still connected. Includes the circuit's detection latency and threshold offsets.

Event log

1x plays 1 s of simulated time per second. Replay of simulation output, not live monitoring.
